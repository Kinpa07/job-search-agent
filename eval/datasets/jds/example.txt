Backend Engineer

About the role
We're hiring a backend engineer to build and maintain our API platform. You'll
work in a small team shipping features end to end, from design through to
deployment and monitoring.

Requirements
- 1-2 years of professional software development experience.
- Proficiency in Python (FastAPI or Django) or another modern backend language.
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
- Working knowledge of a relational database (PostgreSQL or MySQL).
- Comfortable with Git and writing unit/integration tests.

Nice to have
- Docker and CI/CD experience.
- Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS/GCP).
- Familiarity with Redis or other caching layers.
